4.3.6.7 View/Add/Edit Property Picture

If property pictures have been attached to a Loan, a camera icon will appear next to the property
record. Loan Property Pictures can be viewed in the Property Inspection tab where the Active Property
photo will be displayed.

Step 1: — Click on [EpIT ITEM] located in the Actions column of the Property Inspection dashboard to
navigate to Property Inspection (Edit Mode).

Step 2: Scroll to the bottom of the page to view the Property Picture dashboard.

Step 3: To adda picture, click on [ADD PICTURE] located under the Property Pictures title. To edit a
picture, click on [EDIT PicTuRE] located in the Actions column of the Property Pictures
dashboard.

Step 4: Drag and drop a picture or click on the Upload button to upload a file (if you are using
Internet Explorer, you can only click to upload). (Note; Only TIF, JPG, JPEG, PNG, or PDF
files are accepted.)

Step 5: Complete all required fields and click [SAVE].

Step 6: On the Confirmation Pop-up, click [SUBMIT].

After saving, the user redirects to Property Inspection edit mode and if the user scrolls down the
Property Picture dashboard will display with new addition.
