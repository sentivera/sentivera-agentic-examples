1 Solution Summary

This manual is designed to guide Lenders through the use of the Single-Family Mortgage Asset Recovery
Technology (SMART) System.

1.1 Features

The Single-Family Mortgage Asset Recovery Technology (SMART) System is comprised of individual
modules designed to assist the user in searching for specific loan information and performing related
functions. The modules currently available include the following:

Loan Module: Query and review loan information at a detailed level, add new loan records to
the system, and edit existing loan information.

Servicing Module: Assign mortgage servicing templates to individual loans, track the status of
each servicing template, and generate the corresponding servicing forms and letters.
Bankruptcy Module: Maintain, track, and edit bankruptcy information for individual loan
records.

Foreclosure Module: Manage loan records in the foreclosure process. Module provides a
timeline of the required foreclosure steps and tracks the scheduled foreclosure activity vs. the
completed foreclosure activity.

Mortgage Release Module: Initiate the loan release process for satisfied loans, track the release
status, and generate the corresponding release forms and letters.

Cash Management Module: Track and manage incoming payments from Pay.Gov, submit
disbursement checks, search for transactions and review the TROR and Trial Balance reports.
Customer Service Module: Create, view, and manage tickets. Create and edit call logs and ticket
actions.

Maintenance Module: Manage user account, reset user passwords, and update ticket round
robin.
