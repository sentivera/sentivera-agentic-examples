10 Using the Customer Service Module

Within the Customer Service Module, there are two types of tickets: SMART and CRM. SMART tickets
are created using the SMART application. CRM (Customer Relationship Management), tickets are
created in a separate program used to track customer service tickets. CRM tickets are then imported
into SMART. The @ icon will appear before or after the CRM Ticket Number anywhere a Ticket appears
(such as My Tickets, All Inboxes, Ticket Search Screen, and within the Customer Service Module Ticket
Information Screen).

Additionally, there are two types of tickets: sys tickets and non-sys tickets. If a Loan No. exists for a
ticket, then that system is sys. If a Loan No. exists for a ticket, then that system is non-sys.
