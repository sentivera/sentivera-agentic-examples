2.4.1. My Dashboard

The default page you come to after you have logged into SMART is the [My DASHBOARD] page. Under the
tab [My DASHBOARD] you have five options: My Tickets, Supervisor Inbox, HUD Inbox, Action List, and
Unassigned Tickets.

2.4.2 Loan

The [LOAN] tab directs the user to the Loan Module in SMART. Here you have one option: Search.

2.4.3 Servicing

The [SERVICING] tab directs the user to the Servicing Module. Here you have one option: Search.

2.4.4 Customer Service
The [CUSTOMER SERVICE] tab directs the user to the Customer Service Module. Here you have three
options: Ticket Search, Create New Ticket, and CRM Review Queue.

2.4.5 Reports

Under [REPORTS] you have three options: Customer Service, Director Reports, and Servicing/Release
Reports.

2.4.6 User Information and Roles

Select your user name to view and update your personal identification and contact information. You
may also change your password and security questions. From the [ROLE] drop-down menu, select the
role in which to view the SMART System.

2.4.7 Maintenance
The [MAINTENANCE] tab directs the user to the Maintenance Module. Here you have one option: Ticket
Round Robin.
