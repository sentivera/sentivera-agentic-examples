9 Using the Cash Management Module

Within the Cash Management Module, there are two types of transactions: SMART and Pay.Gov.
SMART tickets are created using the SMART application. Pay.Gov transactions are payments that are
made on Pay.Gov and submitted via a validated submission forum to SMART. The © icon will appear
before or after the Pay.Gov Ticket Number anywhere a transaction appears (such as the Transactions
Tab within the Loan Module, Trial Balance - USSGL Account, and the Transaction Search Screen).

Data is refreshed on a nightly basis; however, if users need to refresh the data for today’s transactions,
they can do so from Cash Management. The Cash Management screens will display Transactions as of
the last data refresh.
