4.4 Accessing Detailed Loan Information

Opening a Loan Record from the search results will navigate the user to the Loan Record page. The Loan
Record page consists of multiple tabs that provide detailed Loan information.

Step1: Click [LOAN] from the top Navigation menu. Select [SEARCH] from the dropdown.

Step 2: In the Loan Search Screen, select the Loan Program from the Loan Program drop down.

Step 3: Click [START SEARCH]. 50 records will display in the Search Results Dashboard.

Step 4: From the Search Results Dashboard, click on either the Loan Skey, FHA Case #, and Loan #
to open the loan.

Figure 41: Loan Record (example)

To minimize the loan record, click on the black arrow (+) located left of the Loan Record page title. To
maximize, re-click on the black arrow.

To minimize the Borrower & Property Information and Additional Loan Information, click on the white
arrow (Ez) located left of the of the Borrower & Property information box or the white arrow located left
of the Additional Loan Information. To maximize, re-click on the white arrow.

On each Loan Record page, the selection of tabs displayed is dynamic and varies depending on the Loan
program of the selected loan. The colors of the tabs show at a glance whether data exists in the tab or

not.

Note: Loans that are Closed are all Read Only.
